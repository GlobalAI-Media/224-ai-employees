**PURPOSE**  
This checklist provides a baseline for lawful operations across key SEA markets (Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines). It is not exhaustive and must be supplemented by local counsel advice for each jurisdiction.

---

## A. Corporate & Licensing

- [ ] **Entity Establishment** – Confirm whether a local entity is required (e.g., Indonesia, Vietnam) or if a foreign entity may operate remotely (e.g., Singapore).
- [ ] **Business Licenses** – Obtain relevant e-commerce/trading licenses (e.g., Singapore’s *Online Business Registration*, Malaysia’s *SSM registration*, Thailand’s *e-Commerce license* under the DBD).
- [ ] **Marketplace vs. Retailer** – Determine if you act as a marketplace (platform) or retailer; licensing and liability differ.
- [ ] **Foreign Ownership Caps** – Check equity limits (e.g., Indonesia’s Positive Investment List, Philippines’ Retail Trade Liberalization Act).
