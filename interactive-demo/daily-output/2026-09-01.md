### **1. Route Overview**  
For consumer electronics (high-value, time-sensitive, and requiring careful handling), we recommend a **sea-freight route via the Port of Jebel Ali (Dubai)** as the primary option, with an **air-freight alternative** for urgent shipments. Below is the detailed outline.

---

### **2. Primary Route: Sea Freight (FCL/LCL)**  

**Origin:** Port of Shanghai (Yangshan Deep-Water Port)  
**Destination:** Port of Jebel Ali, Dubai, UAE  
**Transit Time:** 18–22 days (direct) or 25–30 days (via transshipment)  

**Route Details:**  
- **Shipping Line:** Direct service (e.g., Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM) via the **Strait of Malacca → Indian Ocean → Arabian Sea → Gulf of Oman → Strait of Hormuz → Jebel Ali**.  
- **Vessel Type:** Container ship (preferably with reefer plugs if needed for temperature-sensitive components).  
- **Incoterms Recommended:** FOB Shanghai or CIF Jebel Ali (depending on risk allocation).
