### Preliminary Note

Southeast Asia is not a single regulatory market. ASEAN harmonization initiatives (e.g., the ASEAN Agreement on E-Commerce, ATIGA, RCEP) provide a framework, but enforcement remains national. This checklist flags baseline obligations; each market requires local counsel verification before launch.

---

## 1. Market Entry & Corporate Structure

- [ ] Determine permitted foreign ownership thresholds (e.g., Indonesia's Negative Investment List; Vietnam's WTO commitments; Thailand's Foreign Business Act).
- [ ] Confirm whether a local entity, representative office, or distributor arrangement is required.
- [ ] Verify registration with the relevant investment authority (e.g., BKPM in Indonesia, BOI in Thailand, DICA in Myanmar).
- [ ] Assess whether a regional holding structure (commonly Singapore) triggers permanent establishment or transfer pricing exposure.
- [ ] Confirm business licensing for e-commerce specifically (e.g., Indonesia's PSE registration, Vietnam's e-commerce trading floor notification).
