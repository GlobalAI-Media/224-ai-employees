# Cross-Border E-Commerce Compliance Checklist: Southeast Asia

**Prepared by:** Legal Advisor, Global AI Media Group
**Scope:** Market-entry and ongoing compliance for cross-border e-commerce operations across ASEAN markets
**Status:** Working draft for internal review — not legal advice until tailored to specific jurisdictions and business model

---

## 1. Corporate Structure & Market Entry

- [ ] Determine entry vehicle per market (local entity, branch, representative office, or cross-border remote sales)
- [ ] Confirm foreign ownership restrictions (e.g., Indonesia's Negative Investment List; Philippines Retail Trade Liberalization Act thresholds; Thailand Foreign Business Act)
- [ ] Verify whether e-commerce activities require a specific foreign business license
- [ ] Register local entity / obtain tax identification numbers where required
- [ ] Appoint local statutory representatives or directors where mandated
- [ ] Execute intercompany agreements (IP licensing, services, transfer pricing documentation)

## 2. E-Commerce-Specific Licensing & Registration

- [ ] **Indonesia:** Register as PPMSE (electronic system provider) under Permendag 31/2023; assess social-commerce restrictions (separate licensing for social media transactions)
- [ ] **Vietnam:** Notify/register e-commerce website or app with the Ministry of Industry and Trade (Decree 52/2013 as amended); register with the Online Public Service Portal
