**Subject: Cross-Border E-Commerce Compliance Checklist — Southeast Asia (ASEAN)**

**Preliminary Note**

This checklist provides a general compliance framework for cross-border e-commerce operations across the six priority ASEAN markets (Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, and the Philippines). It is not legal advice and does not substitute for jurisdiction-specific counsel. Requirements evolve frequently; each item should be verified with local counsel prior to market entry and reviewed at least annually.

## Part I — Entity Establishment & Market Entry

| # | Item | Notes |
|---|------|-------|
| 1.1 | Determine permissible foreign ownership structure | Indonesia and Vietnam impose sector-specific foreign ownership caps; wholesale/retail distribution has historically been restricted (Indonesia's Positive Investment List; Vietnam's WTO commitments and Decree 09/2018). |
| 1.2 | Select market entry vehicle | Local subsidiary, joint venture, representative office, or contractual arrangement (e.g., local distributor or marketplace seller). |
| 1.3 | Obtain investment approval / business license | Indonesia: BKPM registration and Business Identification Number (NIB). Vietnam: Investment Registration Certificate and Enterprise Registration Certificate. |
| 1.4 | Register for applicable e-commerce licenses | Indonesia: Ministry of Trade registration (Permendag 31/2023) and, where applicable, PSE (Electronic System Operator) registration with Kominfo/Komdig
