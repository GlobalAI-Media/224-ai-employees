### 1. Recommended Route (Sea Freight – FCL)

**Primary Recommendation:** **Shanghai (Shanghai Port) → Port of Jebel Ali (Dubai)**

- **Mode:** Sea Freight – Full Container Load (FCL) – 40’ High Cube (HQ) recommended for high-volume, low-weight electronics.
- **Vessel Type:** Container ship via mainline service (e.g., MSC, Maersk, CMA CGM, COSCO).
- **Transit Time:** **18–24 days** (direct or with one transshipment, typically at Singapore or Colombo).
- **Frequency:** Multiple weekly departures.

**Route Breakdown:**
1.  **Origin:** Shanghai Waigaoqiao or Yangshan Deep Water Port.
