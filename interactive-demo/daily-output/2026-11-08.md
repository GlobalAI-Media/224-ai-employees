## Logistics Coordinator
**Task:** Outline a shipping route from Shanghai to Dubai for consumer electronics.

**Shipping Route Outline: Shanghai → Dubai (Consumer Electronics)**

**1. Origin Handling (Shanghai)**
- Cargo consolidated at a Shanghai warehouse (e.g., Waigaoqiao or Yangshan port area) for inspection, labeling, and export documentation.
- Export customs clearance and booking confirmation with the carrier 7–10 days before vessel departure.

**2. Port of Loading: Shanghai (CNSHA)**
- Primary option: Yangshan Deep-Water Port (containerized electronics).
- Alternative: Waigaoqiao Terminal if schedules align better.

**3. Ocean Freight Leg**
- **Route:** Shanghai → Strait of Malacca → Indian Ocean → Arabian Sea → Strait of Hormuz (or direct to UAE via the Gulf of Oman).
- **Transit time:** Approximately 18–25 days port-to-port, depending on carrier and routing (direct services are faster; transshipment via Singapore or Colombo adds 3–7 days).
- **Carrier options:** Major alliances (e.g., CMA CGM, Maersk, COSCO, MSC) offer regular FCL services; LCL consolidation also available.

**4. Port of Discharge: Jebel Ali (AEJEA), Dubai**
- Jebel Ali is the primary container gateway for Dubai; Port Rashid and Sharjah (Khor Fakkan) are alternatives depending on consignee location.
