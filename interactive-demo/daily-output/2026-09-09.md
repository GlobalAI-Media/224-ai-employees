## Logistics Coordinator
**Task:** Outline a shipping route from Shanghai to Dubai for consumer electronics.

**Subject: Proposed Shipping Route – Consumer Electronics (Shanghai to Dubai)**  

Dear Team,  

Based on our requirements for cost-efficiency, transit time, and cargo safety for consumer electronics, I recommend the following shipping route from Shanghai to Dubai:  

---

### **Recommended Route: Sea Freight (FCL – Full Container Load)**  
**Port of Loading:** Shanghai (Yangshan or Waigaoqiao Terminal)  
**Port of Discharge:** Jebel Ali Port, Dubai (UAE)  

**Transit Time:** 18–22 days (direct service)  
**Carrier Options:** Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, or COSCO (all operate direct sailings on this trade lane)  

---

### **Step-by-Step Routing Plan**  

1. **Inland Haulage (Shanghai)**  
   - Pick up cargo from supplier warehouse (if not already at port).  
   - Transport to Shanghai port via truck (approx. 1–2 days, depending on origin).  
   - Arrange container stuffing at a CFS (Container Freight Station) or at the factory if using door-to-door service.  

2. **Export Customs Clearance (China)**  
   - Submit export declaration, commercial invoice, packing list, and HS code (e.g., 8517 for phones, 8471 for laptops).
