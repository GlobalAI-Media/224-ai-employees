**Preliminary Note**

Southeast Asia is not a single regulatory market. Each ASEAN member state maintains distinct regimes governing trade, taxation, data, consumer protection, and payments, layered atop regional frameworks (AFTA/ATIGA, RCEP, ASEAN Digital Economy Framework Agreement). This checklist is structured by domain, with country-specific flags. It is a general advisory tool and does not substitute for local counsel engagement in each target jurisdiction.

---

## I. Market Entry & Corporate Structure

- [ ] Determine entry vehicle: local subsidiary, branch, joint venture, or distributor/agent arrangement
- [ ] Confirm foreign ownership caps by sector (e.g., Indonesia's Positive Investment List; Vietnam's WTO commitments and conditional sectors; Thailand's Foreign Business Act)
- [ ] Assess nominee arrangement risks — prohibited or unenforceable in most jurisdictions
- [ ] Register with the relevant investment authority where incentives apply (e.g., Indonesia BKPM, Thailand BOI, Vietnam MPI)
- [ ] Obtain business licenses and sector-specific permits (trading, logistics, digital services)
- [ ] Confirm whether a local director, registered office, or resident representative is mandatory

## II. Import, Customs & Trade

- [ ] Classify goods under the ASEAN Harmonized Tariff Nomenclature
