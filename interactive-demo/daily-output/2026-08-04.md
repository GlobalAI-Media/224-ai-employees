### Executive Summary

The Middle East represents a **high-value, strategic growth corridor** for Brazilian sugar exports, specifically for **refined white sugar (ICUMSA 45)** and **raw sugar** destined for local refining. While Brazil is already the world’s largest producer and exporter, the Middle East offers a unique combination of structural demand deficits, geopolitical supply chain shifts, and favorable trade economics. We project a **sustainable CAGR of 4–6%** in import volume over the next five years, contingent on logistics optimization and competitive pricing against European and Indian suppliers.
