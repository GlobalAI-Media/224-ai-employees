## 1. INTRODUCTION & SCOPE

This checklist applies to all e-commerce operations (B2B/B2C, marketplace, or direct-to-consumer) targeting or operating within **ASEAN member states**: Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam, Myanmar, Cambodia, Laos, and Brunei.

**Key Principle:** Compliance is *jurisdictional*, not regional. While ASEAN harmonizes some standards, **local laws prevail**. This checklist is a living document—review quarterly or upon regulatory change.
