## 1. Recommended Primary Route (Sea Freight — FCL)

| Leg | Segment | Details |
|-----|---------|---------|
| 1 | **Origin haulage** | Factory/warehouse (Shanghai or Yangtze Delta) → Shanghai Port (Yangshan Deep-Water Port or Waigaoqiao) |
| 2 | **Export clearance** | Customs declaration, export license check, commodity inspection |
| 3 | **Ocean leg** | Shanghai → Strait of Malacca → Indian Ocean → Gulf of Aden → Strait of Hormuz → Jebel Ali Port, Dubai |
| 4 | **Import clearance** | UAE customs, duty/VAT processing, conformity (ECAS/G-Mark where applicable) |
| 5 | **Final delivery** | Jebel Ali → Dubai consignee or onward GCC distribution |

- **Transit time:** ~18–24 days port-to-port
- **Distance:** ~6,500 nautical miles
- **Service options:** Direct calls or transshipment via Singapore, Colombo, or Salalah
