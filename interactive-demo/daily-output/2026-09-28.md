### Preliminary Note

This checklist provides a general compliance framework for cross-border e-commerce operations across Southeast Asia. It is not legal advice and does not substitute for jurisdiction-specific counsel. SEA is not a single regulatory bloc; each market maintains distinct regimes. The checklist addresses the six major markets (Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines) with notes on emerging markets where relevant.

## I. Entity Establishment & Market Entry

| # | Item | Notes |
|---|------|-------|
| 1.1 | Determine permitted foreign ownership structure | Indonesia: Negative Investment List (max 49–67% in e-commerce distribution); Vietnam: WTO commitments + Decree 09/2018; Thailand: Foreign Business Act (49% cap absent BOI promotion) |
| 1.2 | Register local entity or appoint local representative | Required in Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand for most retail/e-commerce activity |
| 1.3 | Obtain investment approval / business license | Thailand BOI or FBL; Vietnam IRC + ERC; Indonesia BKPM/OSS-RBA |
| 1.4 | Assess availability of regional holding structures | Singapore commonly used as regional HQ; verify treaty benefits and substance requirements |
