# Shipping Route Outline: Shanghai → Dubai (Consumer Electronics)

**Prepared by:** Logistics Coordinator, Global AI Media Group
**Commodity:** Consumer electronics (Class 9 lithium battery considerations apply)

---

## 1. Recommended Primary Route — Ocean Freight (FCL)

**Origin:** Port of Shanghai (Yangshan Deep-Water Port or Waigaoqiao)
**Destination:** Jebel Ali Port, Dubai, UAE

| Leg | Segment | Details |
|-----|---------|---------|
| 1 | Shanghai → Strait of Malacca | Departure via East China Sea, southbound |
| 2 | Malacca Strait → Indian Ocean | Transit Singapore/Malacca |
| 3 | Indian Ocean crossing | Westbound toward Arabian Sea |
| 4 | Arabian Sea → Gulf of Oman | Approach via Strait of Hormuz |
| 5 | Jebel Ali Port | Berthing, customs clearance |

**Transit time:** 18–24 days (direct or single-transshipment services)
**Service options:** Direct calls on CMA CGM, Maersk, COSCO, and ONE strings; common transshipment hubs include Singapore, Port Klang, or Colombo.
