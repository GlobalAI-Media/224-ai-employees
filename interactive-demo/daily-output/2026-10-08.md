## Logistics Coordinator
**Task:** Outline a shipping route from Shanghai to Dubai for consumer electronics.

**Shipping Route Outline: Shanghai → Dubai (Consumer Electronics)**

**1. Route Overview**
- **Origin:** Shanghai, China (Port of Shanghai – Yangshan or Waigaoqiao terminal)
- **Destination:** Dubai, UAE (Jebel Ali Port, primary; Port Rashid as alternative)
- **Mode:** Ocean freight (FCL preferred for consumer electronics), with optional air freight for urgent/high-value batches

**2. Primary Ocean Route**
- **Leg 1 – Shanghai to Singapore:** Depart Yangshan/Waigaoqiao, transit via East China Sea and South China Sea to the Port of Singapore (~5–7 days). Singapore serves as a major transshipment and bunkering hub.
- **Leg 2 – Singapore to Colombo (optional):** Some services call at Colombo, Sri Lanka, for regional consolidation (~3–4 days).
- **Leg 3 – Colombo/Singapore to Jebel Ali:** Cross the Arabian Sea, transit the Strait of Hormuz area (or route south of it depending on carrier), and approach the Persian Gulf to Jebel Ali (~7–10 days).
- **Total transit time:** Typically **18–25 days** port-to-port, depending on carrier, routing, and vessel schedule.

**3. Alternative Routing**
- **Direct service:** Some carriers offer direct Shanghai–Jebel Ali services via the Malacca Strait and Arabian Sea, reducing transit to **~15–18 days**.
