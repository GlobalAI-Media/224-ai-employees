# Cross-Border E-Commerce Compliance Checklist — Southeast Asia

**Prepared by:** Legal & Compliance, Global AI Media Group
**Scope:** Sale of goods/services via digital channels into ASEAN markets (initial focus: Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines)
**Status:** Internal working document — not legal advice to third parties; local counsel sign-off required per jurisdiction (see §9)

---

## 1. Market Entry & Corporate Structure
- [ ] Determine whether local entity, representative office, or remote cross-border sale model is permitted for the product category
- [ ] Confirm foreign ownership restrictions (e.g., Indonesia's Negative Investment List; Thailand's Foreign Business Act)
- [ ] Assess whether a local importer of record / authorized representative is required
- [ ] Register with the relevant e-commerce regulator where mandated (e.g., Thailand's ETDA/DBD, Vietnam's MOIT e-commerce portal, Indonesia's Kominfo/OSS system)

## 2. Product-Specific Licensing & Certification
- [ ] Identify category-specific regimes: food, cosmetics, medical devices, electronics, toys, chemicals
- [ ] Obtain required registrations (e.g., Indonesia BPOM; Thailand FDA; Vietnam MOH/DAV; Malaysia NPRA; Philippines FDA)
- [ ] Confirm labeling language requirements (e.g., Thai-language labels; Bahasa Indonesia labeling)
- [ ] Verify electrical/telecom certification (e.g., Singapore IMDA, Malaysia SIR
