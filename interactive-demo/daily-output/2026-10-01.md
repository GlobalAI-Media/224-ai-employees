## Logistics Coordinator
**Task:** Outline a shipping route from Shanghai to Dubai for consumer electronics.

**Shipping Route Outline: Shanghai → Dubai (Consumer Electronics)**

**1. Origin & Export Handling (Shanghai)**
- Cargo consolidated at a Shanghai warehouse; consumer electronics packed with anti-static/ESD protection and moisture barriers.
- Export customs clearance and booking confirmation with the freight forwarder.
- Goods moved to **Port of Shanghai (Yangshan or Waigaoqiao terminal)** for loading.

**2. Primary Ocean Leg**
- **Route:** Shanghai → Strait of Malacca → Indian Ocean → Arabian Sea → Strait of Hormuz → Persian Gulf → **Jebel Ali Port, Dubai**.
- **Transit time:** approximately 18–25 days, depending on carrier, routing, and whether the service is direct or transshipment-based.
- **Common transshipment hubs** if not direct: Singapore, Port Klang, or Colombo.
- Major carriers on this lane include Maersk, CMA CGM, COSCO, and Hapag-Lloyd.

**3. Destination & Import Handling (Dubai)**
- Vessel berths at Jebel Ali (or Port Rashid for certain cargo).
- Import customs clearance with Dubai Customs; ensure HS codes, certificates of origin, and any conformity documents (e.g., ESMA/ECAS for electronics) are prepared.
- Deconsolidation and final delivery via road to Jebel Ali Free Zone, Dubai Industrial City, or local distributors.
