## Logistics Coordinator
**Task:** Outline a shipping route from Shanghai to Dubai for consumer electronics.

**Shipping Route Outline: Shanghai → Dubai (Consumer Electronics)**

**1. Mode Selection**
- **Primary recommendation:** Ocean freight (FCL) — cost-effective for volume consumer electronics; transit ~18–25 days port-to-port.
- **Alternative:** Air freight (PVG → DXB) for high-value/time-sensitive SKUs; transit 1–3 days.

**2. Ocean Freight Routing (FCL)**
- **Origin port:** Port of Shanghai (Yangshan or Waigaoqiao terminal)
- **Carrier options:** CMA CGM, COSCO, Maersk, MSC (direct or transshipment services)
- **Routing:** Direct services via Malacca Strait → Indian Ocean → Arabian Sea to Jebel Ali Port, Dubai. Some services transship via Singapore, Colombo, or Salalah depending on carrier schedule.
- **Transit time:** ~18–22 days direct; ~22–28 days with transshipment
- **Destination port:** Jebel Ali (primary) or Port Rashid

**3. Inland & Final Mile**
- Jebel Ali → Jebel Ali Free Zone (JAFZA) or Dubai mainland via bonded trucking
- Customs clearance at Dubai Customs; DEWA/ESMA compliance for electronics (ECAS registration where applicable)
