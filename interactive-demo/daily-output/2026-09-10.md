### Preliminary Note

This checklist covers the six major SEA markets (Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines). Requirements vary significantly by jurisdiction — particularly regarding foreign ownership, data localization, and product registration. **This is a general framework; market-specific legal opinions should be obtained before launch in each jurisdiction.**

---

## 1. Corporate Structure & Market Entry

- [ ] Determine permitted foreign ownership level for e-commerce activities in target market (e.g., Indonesia's negative investment list; Vietnam's WTO commitments; Thailand's Foreign Business Act)
- [ ] Select entry vehicle: wholly foreign-owned entity, joint venture, local distributor/reseller, or marketplace arrangement
- [ ] Confirm required licenses (e.g., Indonesia: NIB + PSE registration; Vietnam: e-commerce trading floor license; Thailand: e-commerce business registration with DBD)
- [ ] Appoint local representative or registered agent where required
- [ ] Register for local tax identification (TIN/NPWP/etc.) and VAT/GST where applicable

## 2. Product Compliance & Market Access
