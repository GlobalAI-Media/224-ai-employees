# Cross-Border E-Commerce Compliance Checklist — Southeast Asia

**Prepared by:** Legal & Compliance, Global AI Media Group
**Scope:** Sale of goods/services via digital channels into and within ASEAN markets (initial focus: Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines)
**Status:** Working draft for internal review — not legal advice; local counsel validation required per market

---

## 1. Market Entry & Corporate Structure
- [ ] Determine market entry model (direct cross-border sales, local entity, marketplace seller, distributor/JV)
- [ ] Confirm foreign ownership restrictions (e.g., Indonesia Negative Investment List; Philippines Retail Trade Liberalization Act thresholds)
- [ ] Register local entity/branch or appoint local representative where required
- [ ] Obtain business licenses (e.g., Indonesia NIB via OSS; Thailand Foreign Business License where applicable; Vietnam Investment Registration Certificate/ERC)
- [ ] Confirm tax registration (corporate income tax, VAT/GST) in each market

## 2. Product Compliance & Restricted Goods
- [ ] Classify products against each market's prohibited/restricted lists (e.g., Vietnam's banned goods list; Indonesia import restrictions under Permendag 36/2023 as amended)
- [ ] Obtain product certifications (e.g., Indonesia SNI; Thailand TISI; Malaysia SIRIM; Philippines ICC/PS marks)
- [ ] Verify labeling requirements (local language, origin, warnings — e.g., Thai FDA
