# Cross-Border E-Commerce Compliance Checklist — Southeast Asia

**Prepared by: Legal Advisor, Global AI Media Group**
**Scope: Sale of physical/digital goods via e-commerce into ASEAN markets (focus: Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines)**
**Status: Internal working document — not legal advice to third parties; local counsel confirmation required per jurisdiction**

---

## 1. Entity & Market Entry

- [ ] Determine market entry structure: direct cross-border sales, local entity, or marketplace seller arrangement
- [ ] Confirm foreign ownership restrictions (e.g., Indonesia's Negative Investment List; Thailand's Foreign Business Act)
- [ ] Register local entity/branch where required (e.g., Vietnam: e-commerce sales to consumers may require local presence or representative)
- [ ] Appoint local representative/agent where mandated (e.g., Indonesia: PMSE registration requires local representative for foreign operators)
- [ ] Obtain business licenses (e.g., Indonesia: NIB via OSS system; Singapore: ACRA registration if local entity)

## 2. E-Commerce Platform & Operator Registration

- [ ] Register as e-commerce operator with the relevant regulator:
  - Indonesia: Kominfo/MoC PMSE registration (Permenkominfo 5/2020)
  - Vietnam: MoIT e-commerce portal registration (Decree 52/2013, as amended)
  - Thailand: ETDA/DIT registration where applicable
