## Logistics Coordinator
**Task:** Outline a shipping route from Shanghai to Dubai for consumer electronics.

**Shipping Route Outline: Shanghai → Dubai (Consumer Electronics)**

**1. Origin Handling (Shanghai)**
- **Pickup & Consolidation:** Cargo collected from supplier facilities and consolidated at a bonded warehouse in the Shanghai free-trade zone (e.g., Waigaoqiao or Yangshan).
- **Export Customs Clearance:** Declared under the appropriate HS codes for consumer electronics; note lithium-battery items (UN3480/UN3481) require MSDS and dangerous-goods documentation if applicable.
- **Port of Loading:** Shanghai (Yangshan Deep-Water Port or Waigaoqiao) — selected based on carrier service and vessel schedule.

**2. Ocean Freight Leg**
- **Primary Route:** Shanghai → Strait of Malacca → Indian Ocean → Arabian Sea → Jebel Ali Port, Dubai.
- **Transit Time:** Approximately 18–25 days port-to-port, depending on carrier and routing (direct services are typically ~18–20 days; transshipment via Singapore or Colombo may extend this).
- **Carrier Options:** Major alliances (Maersk/MSC, CMA CGM, COSCO, Evergreen) offer regular FCL services; LCL consolidation also available via NVOCCs.
- **Cargo Protection:** Electronics shipped in FCL containers with desiccant and moisture-barrier packaging; consider GPS-enabled containers for high-value consignments.
