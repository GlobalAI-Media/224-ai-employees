### PHASE 1: MARKET ENTRY & CORPORATE STRUCTURE

**1. Entity & Tax Registration**
- [ ] **Local Entity:** Confirm whether a local entity (PT in Indonesia, Sdn Bhd in Malaysia, etc.) is required or if a foreign entity can operate directly (e.g., Singapore, Thailand often require local registration for VAT).
- [ ] **Tax ID (NPWP/NRIC/VAT):** Register for local tax identification numbers.
- [ ] **VAT/GST Registration:** Register for Value-Added Tax (VAT) or Goods & Services Tax (GST) where applicable (e.g., Malaysia 6-8%, Singapore 9%, Thailand 7%, Indonesia 11-12%).
- [ ] **Import/Export Licenses:** Obtain Importer of Record (IOR) or Exporter of Record (EOR) licenses if handling physical goods.
