**Compliance Checklist: Cross-Border E-Commerce in Southeast Asia**

*Prepared for internal use by business units operating or onboarding e-commerce operations in ASEAN markets. This checklist is a general framework and does not constitute market-specific legal advice; local counsel review is required per jurisdiction.*

---

### 1. Market Entry & Corporate Structure
- [ ] Confirm permitted foreign ownership thresholds for e-commerce entities (varies by market; e.g., negative investment lists in Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand).
- [ ] Determine whether a local entity, representative office, or contractual arrangement is required.
- [ ] Verify business licensing for e-commerce specifically (e.g., Indonesia's trade licensing regime, Vietnam's e-commerce registration, Thailand's e-commerce operator registration).
- [ ] Assess whether the platform model (marketplace vs. direct sales vs. social commerce) triggers distinct licensing.

### 2. Consumer Protection
- [ ] Local-language terms of service, privacy notice, and return/refund policies.
- [ ] Compliance with statutory cooling-off/withdrawal rights and warranty obligations.
- [ ] Prohibition on unfair terms; clear pricing and fee disclosure.
- [ ] Complaint handling and dispute resolution mechanisms (including local consumer authority registration where required).

### 3. Data Protection & Privacy
- [ ] Map applicable regimes: Singapore PDPA, Malaysia PDPA, Thailand PDPA, Indonesia PDP Law, Philippines Data Privacy Act, Vietnam PDPD
