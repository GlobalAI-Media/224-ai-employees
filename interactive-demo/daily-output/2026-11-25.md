## Logistics Coordinator
**Task:** Outline a shipping route from Shanghai to Dubai for consumer electronics.

**Subject: Proposed Shipping Route – Consumer Electronics, Shanghai to Dubai**

Thank you for your inquiry. Below is a recommended logistics plan for moving consumer electronics from Shanghai to Dubai, balancing transit time, cost, and cargo security.

**1. Recommended Route: Ocean Freight (FCL) via Shanghai → Jebel Ali**

- **Origin:** Port of Shanghai (Yangshan or Waigaoqiao terminal), China
- **Destination:** Jebel Ali Port, Dubai, UAE
- **Carrier options:** Major alliances (Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, COSCO) offer weekly direct sailings
- **Transit time:** Approximately 18–24 days port-to-port, depending on carrier and routing (direct services via the Strait of Malacca and Indian Ocean are typical; some services transship via Singapore or Colombo)
- **Container type:** 20ft or 40ft HC FCL recommended for volume shipments; LCL consolidation available for smaller lots

**2. Alternative Options**

- **Air freight (PVG → DXB):** 1–3 days transit; recommended for high-value, time-sensitive SKUs or launch inventory. Higher cost per kg.
- **Sea-Air via Singapore:** ~14–18 days; a middle-ground option if ocean transit is too slow but air freight is cost-prohibitive.
- **Rail (China–Europe–Gulf):** Longer and less established for UAE-bound electronics; not recommended as
