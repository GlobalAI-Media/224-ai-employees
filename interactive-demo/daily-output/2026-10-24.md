# Cross-Border E-Commerce Compliance Checklist — Southeast Asia

**Prepared by:** Legal & Compliance Department, Global AI Media Group
**Scope:** Sale of goods/services via digital channels into ASEAN markets
**Status:** Internal working document — not legal advice; local counsel review required per jurisdiction

---

## 1. Market Entry & Corporate Structure
- [ ] Determine market entry vehicle (local entity, representative office, distributor, or direct-to-consumer from abroad)
- [ ] Confirm foreign ownership restrictions in target sector (e.g., negative investment lists in Indonesia, Vietnam, Philippines)
- [ ] Register local entity/branch where required; obtain tax and statistical registration numbers
- [ ] Appoint local data protection representative where required (e.g., Indonesia, Vietnam)
- [ ] Verify WTO/ASEAN commitments and any sector-specific licensing (telecoms, fintech, health products)

## 2. Product Compliance & Standards
- [ ] Classify products under each country's regulated categories (food, cosmetics, medical devices, electronics, children's products)
- [ ] Obtain product registration/notification (e.g., Indonesia BPOM, Thailand FDA, Vietnam MOH, Philippines FDA, Malaysia NPRA)
- [ ] Confirm conformity with national standards and certification (e.g., Indonesia SNI, Vietnam CR mark, Thailand TISI, Malaysia SIRIM)
- [ ] Verify labeling requirements: local language, importer identity, warnings, expiry dates
