### 1. Executive Summary
Brazil is the world’s largest producer and exporter of sugar, with a well-established logistics infrastructure and a competitive cost base. The Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region is a structurally deficit sugar market, importing over 80% of its consumption needs. This presents a **high-potential, long-term strategic opportunity** for Brazilian exporters, provided they navigate regional trade barriers, logistical costs, and evolving consumer preferences.

---

### 2. Market Demand Analysis (Middle East)
- **Consumption Drivers:** The MENA region has a young, growing population, high per-capita sugar consumption (above global average), and a booming food & beverage, confectionery, and bakery sector. Key markets: Saudi Arabia, UAE, Egypt, Iraq, and Yemen.
- **Import Dependency:** The region produces minimal raw sugar (mainly Egypt and Iran), relying heavily on imports. The UAE and Saudi Arabia serve as re-export hubs to East Africa and Central Asia.
