### 1. Recommended Route Overview
For consumer electronics (high value, time-sensitive, moderate volume), I recommend a **sea-air hybrid** or **direct sea freight** depending on your delivery deadline and cost tolerance. Below are two primary options, with the **sea-air route** as the most balanced choice for cost and speed.

---

### 2. Option A – Sea-Air Hybrid (Recommended for Cost-Efficiency + Speed)
- **Origin:** Shanghai Port (or Shanghai Pudong Int’l Airport for air leg)  
- **Sea Leg:** Shanghai → Port Kelang (Malaysia) or Singapore (transshipment hub)  
- **Transit Time (Sea):** 5–7 days  
- **Air Leg:** Port Kelang/Singapore → Dubai International Airport (DXB) or Al Maktoum Int’l (DWC)  
- **Air Transit Time:** 1–2 days  
- **Total Transit Time:** **7–9 days** (door-to-door)  
- **Cost:** ~40–50% less than pure air freight, ~30–40% more than pure sea freight  

**Why this works:**  
- Avoids the high cost of direct air freight from Shanghai.
