# Shipping Route Outline: Shanghai → Dubai (Consumer Electronics)

**Prepared by: Logistics Coordination, Global AI Media Group**

Below is a recommended multimodal routing for consumer electronics (high-value, moisture- and shock-sensitive goods) moving from Shanghai to Dubai.

---

## 1. Recommended Primary Route — Ocean Freight (FCL)

**Origin:** Port of Shanghai (Yangshan or Waigaoqiao terminal)
**Destination:** Jebel Ali Port, Dubai

| Leg | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Inland/Origin handling** | Factory pickup → consolidation at Shanghai warehouse → export customs clearance |
| **Ocean transit** | Shanghai → Strait of Malacca → Indian Ocean → Strait of Hormuz → Jebel Ali |
| **Transit time** | Approx. 18–25 days port-to-port (direct or one-transshipment services) |
| **Carrier options** | Major alliances offer direct or Singapore/Colombo-transshipment services |
| **Destination handling** | Import customs clearance, duty payment, deconsolidation, final-mile delivery to Dubai/Jebel Ali Free Zone |

**Container recommendation:** 40' HC containers with desiccant packs and shock indicators; electronics benefit from humidity control given Gulf climate conditions.
