**Compliance Checklist: Cross-Border E-Commerce in Southeast Asia**

*Prepared by: Legal Advisor | For internal use by business units operating or expanding in SEA markets*

---

**1. Market Entry & Corporate Structure**

- [ ] Confirm foreign investment restrictions and permitted ownership ratios in each target jurisdiction (e.g., Indonesia's Negative Investment List, Vietnam's WTO commitments, Thailand's Foreign Business Act).
- [ ] Determine required entity type (representative office, local subsidiary, joint venture) and complete incorporation/registration.
- [ ] Obtain necessary business licenses (e.g., Indonesia's NIB via OSS system; Thailand's FBL where applicable).
- [ ] Appoint local representatives or directors where mandated.

**2. E-Commerce Platform & Registration Requirements**

- [ ] Register with relevant e-commerce regulators (e.g., Thailand's ETDA, Vietnam's MIC, Indonesia's Kominfo/MoC).
- [ ] Comply with platform notification/licensing regimes for online marketplaces (e.g., Vietnam Decree 52/2013 as amended; Indonesia MoT Regulation 31/2023).
- [ ] File required notifications for website/app operation and domain registration (.co.id, .co.th, .vn, etc. may require local presence).
- [ ] Ensure electronic transaction and e-signature compliance (e.g., Singapore ETA, Malaysia ECA 2024).
