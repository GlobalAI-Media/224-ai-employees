# Cross-Border E-Commerce Compliance Checklist — Southeast Asia

**Prepared by: Legal & Compliance Department, Global AI Media Group**
**Scope: B2C/B2B2C e-commerce operations across ASEAN markets (focus: Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines)**
**Status: Working draft for internal review — not legal advice; local counsel sign-off required per jurisdiction**

---

## 1. Entity & Market Entry

- [ ] Determine market entry structure per country (local entity, representative office, distribution partner, or cross-border direct)
- [ ] Confirm foreign ownership restrictions (e.g., Indonesia negative investment list; Vietnam WTO commitments; Philippines Foreign Investment Act thresholds)
- [ ] Verify business licensing (e.g., Indonesia NIB via OSS system; Thailand Foreign Business License where applicable; Vietnam Investment Registration Certificate / Enterprise Registration Certificate)
- [ ] Appoint local registered agent / company secretary where required
- [ ] Register trade name and trademarks with national IP offices (consider Madrid System designations)

## 2. E-Commerce Platform & Consumer Protection

- [ ] Publish legally required disclosures: seller identity, registered address, contact details, complaint channels
- [ ] Implement country-specific cooling-off / withdrawal rights (e.g., Thailand 7-day return; Indonesia 14-day rights under consumer protection rules)
- [ ] Adopt compliant T&Cs, privacy notices, and refund/return policies in local languages (Bahasa Indonesia, Thai
