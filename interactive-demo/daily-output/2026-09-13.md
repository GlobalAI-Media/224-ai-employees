**1. Recommended Route (Primary Option – Ocean Freight)**

| Leg | Segment | Mode | Est. Transit Time |
|-----|---------|------|-------------------|
| 1 | Shanghai (Yangshan Port) → Singapore | Ocean | 5–7 days |
| 2 | Singapore → Jebel Ali, Dubai | Ocean | 12–15 days |
| 3 | Jebel Ali → Jebel Ali Free Zone / Dubai inland | Truck | 1–2 days |

**Total door-to-door estimate: 20–26 days**

**Routing rationale:**
- Yangshan Port offers the strongest schedule frequency for electronics exports from the Yangtze River Delta.
- Singapore serves as a natural transshipment hub with reliable feeder and mainline connections to the Gulf.
- Jebel Ali is the region's dominant container port and offers direct access to the Jebel Ali Free Zone (JAFZA), which is advantageous for customs and distribution into the GCC.

**Alternative routing:** Direct Shanghai → Jebel Ali services (e.g., via major carriers) can reduce total transit to **16–20 days**, though schedule frequency is lower. Recommended as the preferred option if cargo volume justifies a direct booking.
