**Compliance Checklist: Cross-Border E-Commerce in Southeast Asia**

*Prepared by: Legal Advisor | Distribution: Commercial, Operations, Product, Marketing, Data Protection Leads*

---

### 1. Market Entry & Corporate Structure
- [ ] Determine legal entity structure per market (local subsidiary vs. representative office vs. appointment of local distributor/agent)
- [ ] Confirm foreign ownership restrictions (e.g., Indonesia's Negative Investment List, Thailand's Foreign Business Act, Philippines Retail Trade Liberalization Act thresholds)
- [ ] Verify business licensing requirements (e.g., Indonesia's risk-based licensing under the new Investment Law, Vietnam's e-commerce trading floor registration)
- [ ] Register with relevant e-commerce regulators (e.g., Thailand's ETDA, Malaysia's MDEC/Suruhanjaya Syarikat Malaysia, Indonesia's Kominfo/MoC)
- [ ] Appoint local legal counsel and tax representative per jurisdiction

### 2. Consumer Protection & E-Commerce-Specific Rules
- [ ] Review consumer protection statutes per market (e.g., Malaysia's Consumer Protection Act 1999, Indonesia's Consumer Protection Law No. 8/1999, Thailand's Consumer Protection Act)
