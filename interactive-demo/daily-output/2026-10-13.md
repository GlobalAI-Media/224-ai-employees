**Compliance Checklist: Cross-Border E-Commerce in Southeast Asia**

*Prepared for internal use by business units operating or launching e-commerce operations in ASEAN markets. This checklist is a general framework and does not constitute legal advice for any specific transaction; local counsel review is required before market entry.*

---

### 1. Market Entry & Corporate Structure

- [ ] Determine permitted foreign ownership limits for the relevant e-commerce activity in each target country (varies significantly across ASEAN; some markets require local partners or joint ventures for certain retail/distribution licenses).
- [ ] Select and register the appropriate local entity (subsidiary, branch, or representative office) and obtain the business/trade license.
- [ ] Confirm whether a separate e-commerce-specific license or registration is required (e.g., registration as an e-commerce operator with the relevant ministry or regulator).
- [ ] Appoint a local registered address and, where required, a resident director or authorized representative.
- [ ] Assess whether investment incentives, special economic zone status, or free trade zone treatment apply.

### 2. Licensing & Sector-Specific Permits

- [ ] Identify all activity-based licenses: retail, wholesale, import/export, distribution, logistics, warehousing.
- [ ] Obtain product-specific permits for regulated categories (food, cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, medical devices, electronics, telecom equipment).
- [ ] Confirm advertising and promotional permits where required.
