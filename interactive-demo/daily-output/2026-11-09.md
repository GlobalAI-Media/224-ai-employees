# Cross-Border E-Commerce Compliance Checklist: Southeast Asia

**Prepared by:** Legal Department, Global AI Media Group
**Scope:** Sale of physical goods and digital products/services into ASEAN markets (focus: Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines)
**Status:** Working draft for internal review — not legal advice; local counsel sign-off required per jurisdiction

---

## 1. Market Entry & Corporate Structure
- [ ] Determine whether local entity, representative office, or remote cross-border sale model is permitted for each product category
- [ ] Assess foreign ownership restrictions (e.g., Indonesia's Negative Investment List; Thailand's Foreign Business Act)
- [ ] Confirm whether a local importer of record or authorized representative is required
- [ ] Register for local tax identification where mandatory (e.g., Indonesia VAT registration for foreign digital/e-commerce sellers; Vietnam e-commerce tax registration)
- [ ] Verify appointment of a local data protection representative where required (see §6)

## 2. Product Classification & Restricted Goods
- [ ] Classify all SKUs against each country's prohibited/restricted import lists
- [ ] Screen for category-specific regimes: food, cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, medical devices, supplements, electronics, toys, chemicals
- [ ] Confirm licensing/notification requirements (e.g., Indonesia BPOM; Thailand FDA; Malaysia NPRA; Philippines FDA; Vietnam MOH)
- [ ] Verify labeling language requirements (e.g., Thai-language labeling; Bahasa
