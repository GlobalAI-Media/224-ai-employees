## Logistics Coordinator
**Task:** Outline a shipping route from Shanghai to Dubai for consumer electronics.

**Shipping Route Outline: Shanghai → Dubai (Consumer Electronics)**

**1. Route Overview**
- **Origin:** Shanghai, China (Port of Shanghai – Yangshan or Waigaoqiao terminal)
- **Destination:** Dubai, UAE (Jebel Ali Port, primary; Port Rashid as alternative)
- **Primary mode:** Ocean freight (FCL preferred for consumer electronics)
- **Estimated transit time:** 18–24 days port-to-port

**2. Recommended Route**
- **Leg 1 – Shanghai to Singapore:** Depart Yangshan/Waigaoqiao, transit via East China Sea and South China Sea to the Port of Singapore (~5–7 days). Singapore serves as a major transshipment and bunkering hub.
- **Leg 2 – Singapore to Colombo (optional):** Some carriers route via Colombo, Sri Lanka (~4–5 days) for consolidation or transshipment.
- **Leg 3 – Colombo/Singapore to Jebel Ali:** Cross the Indian Ocean, pass through the Arabian Sea, and enter the Persian Gulf via the Strait of Hormuz to Jebel Ali (~10–12 days).
- **Alternative routing:** Direct services via Malacca Strait → Arabian Sea → Jebel Ali, avoiding Colombo, can reduce handling but may have less frequent sailings.
