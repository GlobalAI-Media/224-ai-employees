## Logistics Coordinator
**Task:** Outline a shipping route from Shanghai to Dubai for consumer electronics.

**Shipping Route Outline: Shanghai → Dubai (Consumer Electronics)**

**1. Origin Handling (Shanghai)**
- **Pickup & Consolidation:** Goods collected from suppliers in the Yangtze River Delta, consolidated at a bonded warehouse near Yangshan Port or Waigaoqiao.
- **Export Customs Clearance:** Declare under appropriate HS codes (e.g., 8517/8471 for electronics). Consumer electronics often require CCC certification for China export compliance, plus MSDS for lithium batteries.
- **Port of Loading:** Shanghai Yangshan Deep-Water Port (or Waigaoqiao Terminal).

**2. Main Carriage – Sea Freight (Primary Option)**
- **Route:** Shanghai → Strait of Malacca → Indian Ocean → Strait of Hormuz → Jebel Ali Port, Dubai.
- **Transit Time:** Approx. 18–25 days depending on service and vessel routing.
- **Carriers:** Major lines include Maersk, CMA CGM, COSCO, and Hapag-Lloyd.
- **Service Type:** FCL (20'/40' containers) preferred for volume; LCL available for smaller batches.

**3. Alternative – Air Freight (Time-Sensitive/High-Value)**
- **Route:** Shanghai Pudong (PVG) → Dubai International (DXB) or Al Maktoum (DWC).
