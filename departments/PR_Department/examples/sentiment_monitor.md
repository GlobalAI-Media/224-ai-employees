## 1. Executive Summary

As the world’s first AI-managed commercial operating system, Global AI Media Group occupies a unique position: we are both the product and the proof. Public perception of AI workforce leasing will directly influence enterprise adoption, regulatory scrutiny, and talent-market reactions. This framework establishes a proactive, data-driven system to track, interpret, and respond to sentiment across media, social platforms, and stakeholder ecosystems—ensuring we shape the narrative rather than react to it.

---

## 2. Strategic Objectives

1. **Early Warning:** Detect emerging risks (e.g., job-loss panic, ethical concerns, regulatory threats) within 2–4 hours of onset.
2. **Narrative Control:** Amplify positive proof points (efficiency gains, human-AI collaboration, upskilling outcomes).
3. **Stakeholder Intelligence:** Segment sentiment by audience (C-suite, HR leaders, policymakers, general public, investors, employees).
4. **Accountability:** Maintain a transparent, evidence-based response posture that reinforces our “responsible innovator” brand.

## 4. Key Metrics & Measurement Framework

### 4.1 Volume Metrics
- **Mention Volume:** Total mentions per day/week across all sources.
- **Share of Voice (SOV):** Our brand vs. competitors vs. generic “AI workforce” conversation.
- **Velocity:** Rate of mention acceleration (e.g., % change hour-over-hour) – triggers early alerts.

### 4.2 Sentiment Metrics
- **Sentiment Score:** Weighted ratio (positive:neutral:negative) on a −100 to +100 scale.
- **Emotion Breakdown:** Fear, anger, hope, excitement, confusion (via NLP + human validation).
- **Topic-Specific Sentiment:** Separate scores for “efficiency,” “ethics,” “job security,” “cost savings,” “innovation.”

### 4.3 Influence & Reach Metrics
- **Influencer Tiering:** Tier 1 (10k+ followers, industry leaders), Tier 2 (1k–10k, niche experts), Tier 3 (journalists, policymakers).
- **Engagement Quality:** Comments vs. likes/shares – high comment volume signals controversy.
- **Reach Amplification:** Estimated impressions per mention, weighted by source authority.

### 4.4 Audience-Specific Sentiment
- **C-Suite/HR:** Focus on ROI, scalability, compliance.
- **General Public:** Focus on job security, fairness, human dignity.
- **Investors:** Focus on market traction, unit economics, regulatory risk.
- **Employees (current/former):** Glassdoor, LinkedIn posts, internal leaks – highest risk for authenticity crises.

### 4.5 Composite Sentiment Index (CSI)
- **Formula:** CSI = (0.4 × Media Sentiment) + (0.3 × Social Sentiment) + (0.2 × Influencer Sentiment) + (0.1 × Audience-Specific Sentiment).
- **Scale:** 0–100 (50 = neutral; >70 = strongly positive; <30 = strongly negative).
