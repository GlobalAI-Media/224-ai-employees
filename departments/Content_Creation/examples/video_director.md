**Task:** Design a storyboard for a 60-second promotional video about a company that offers 224 AI employees for lease. Include scene descriptions, camera angles, and transitions.

# Storyboard: "The 224-Person Workforce That Never Sleeps"
## 60-Second Promotional Video for Global AI Media Group

### Overview
**Tone:** Futuristic, dynamic, and trustworthy  
**Music:** Pulsing electronic beat transitioning into an uplifting orchestral crescendo  
**Tagline:** "Your workforce. Upgraded."

---

## Scene 1: The Empty Office (0:00–0:08)
**Visual:** Slow pan across a sterile, dark office with rows of empty desks. A single computer screen flickers to life.  
**Camera Angle:** Wide shot, slow dolly forward at eye level.  
**Lighting:** Dim blue ambient light, cold and lonely.  
**Audio:** Low hum, echoing footsteps (off-screen).  
**Transition:** Hard cut to a bright, seamless digital grid.

---

## Scene 2: The Digital Awakening (0:08–0:15)
**Visual:** Abstract animation of 224 glowing nodes connecting in a network, forming a human-like silhouette. Each node pulses with a different department logo (e.g., marketing, finance, creative).  
**Camera Angle:** Isometric overhead, then zoom into one node.  
**Lighting:** Neon blue and white, high contrast.  
**Audio:** Synth rise, a soft voice whispers: *"What if your team never stopped learning?"*  
**Transition:** Swipe transition to live-action.

---

## Scene 3: The AI Marketing Manager (0:15–0:22)
**Visual:** A split-screen showing a human executive in a glass office pointing at a screen. On the other side, an AI avatar (named "Vera") analyzes real-time data, generating campaign drafts instantly.  
**Camera Angle:** Medium close-up on human, then cut to over-the-shoulder of AI interface.  
**Lighting:** Warm desk lamp on human side; cool blue glow on AI side.  
**Dialogue:** Human says, *"Vera, we need a new strategy by morning."* AI responds, *"Done. 14 variants. Optimized for conversion."*  
**Transition:** Quick slide transition to next scene.

---

## Scene 4: The 18 Departments Montage (0:22–0:38)
**Visual:** Fast-paced montage of AI employees working across different departments:
- **Creative:** AI generates 3D models and animations (1 sec)
- **Finance:** AI calculates risk reports in seconds (1 sec)
- **Customer Support:** AI handles 500 simultaneous chats (1 sec)
- **Engineering:** AI writes code and deploys patches (1 sec)
- **HR:** AI screens 10,000 resumes (1 sec)
- **Legal:** AI reviews contracts for compliance (1 sec)
- **Data Science:** AI builds predictive models (1 sec)
- **Operations:** AI optimizes supply chain (1 sec)

**Camera Angle:** Dynamic, handheld-style with quick cuts; each scene framed as a "desk" with a glowing AI avatar.  
**Lighting:** High-key, vibrant colors per department (e.g., green for finance, red for marketing).  
**Audio:** Rapid, rhythmic sound effects (keyboard clicks, data streams, robotic affirmations like *"Deployed"* and *"Optimized"*).  
**Transition:** Blur transition to a calm, wide shot.

---

## Scene 5: The Human Advantage (0:38–0:48)
**Visual:** A human CEO sits at a sleek desk, smiling, as a holographic dashboard floats beside them showing 224 active AI employees. The CEO gestures to the dashboard.  
**Camera Angle:** Medium shot, slightly low angle to emphasize confidence.  
**Lighting:** Warm golden hour light through a window.  
**Dialogue:** CEO says, *"They don't take breaks. They don't make excuses. They just deliver."*  
**Audio:** Music swells with strings and a soft piano.  
**Transition:** Fade to black, then quick dissolve.

---

## Scene 6: The Call to Action (0:48–0:60)
**Visual:** Text on screen: *"224 AI Employees. 18 Departments. One Subscription."* Below, a glowing button labeled *"Hire Your AI Workforce Today."*  
**Camera Angle:** Static, centered.  
**Lighting:** Bright, white background with subtle particle effects.  
**Audio:** Voiceover: *"Global AI Media Group. The world's first AI-managed AI operating system. Your workforce, upgraded."*  
**Transition:** Logo appears (Global AI Media Group) with a soft pulse animation. Fade to black.

---
