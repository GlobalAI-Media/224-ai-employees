## funding_progress_tracker
**Task:** Design a fundraising progress dashboard template for tracking investor outreach, meeting stages, term sheet negotiations, and closing timelines for a Series A round.

**INTERNAL MEMORANDUM — INVESTOR RELATIONS DEPARTMENT**  
**TO:** Executive Leadership / Cap Table Committee  
**FROM:** IR Department — Funding Progress Tracker  
**SUBJECT:** Series A Fundraising Progress Dashboard — Template & Operational Framework  

---

### I. Executive Summary

Global AI Media Group is initiating its Series A round with a target raise of **$18M** (primary) plus a **$4M** greenshoe option, at a pre-money valuation of **$120M**. The dashboard below serves as the single source of truth for tracking pipeline velocity, meeting quality, term sheet progression, and capital commitment timing. It is designed to support a 16-week close cycle, with weekly cadence reviews and real-time updates from the IR team.

---

### II. Dashboard Architecture — Key Metrics & Visual Layers

The dashboard is structured across **five core modules**, each with its own KPIs, data sources, and refresh frequency.

#### Module 1: Investor Outreach & Pipeline Health
- **Total Target Investors Contacted:** 240 (across Tier 1 VCs, corporate strategic funds, sovereign-linked LPs, and select family offices)
- **Outreach Conversion Rate:** 38% (target: ≥45% by Week 6)
- **Pipeline by Tier:**
  - Tier 1 (Lead potential, $2M+): 14 investors
  - Tier 2 (Meaningful interest, $500K–$2M): 32 investors
  - Tier 3 (Exploratory, <$500K): 58 investors
- **Active Conversations:** 104 (as of Week 4)

**Visual:** Funnel chart with stage conversion rates (Contacted → Meeting Scheduled → Meeting Completed → Diligence → Term Sheet → Signed).

---

#### Module 2: Meeting & Engagement Stage Tracking
Each investor is tracked through a **7-stage lifecycle**, with expected dwell time per stage:

| Stage | Description | Avg. Dwell Time | Current Count | Conversion to Next Stage |
|-------|-------------|----------------|---------------|--------------------------|
| 1. Initial Outreach | Email/LinkedIn intro | 3 days | 240 | 60% |
| 2. Intro Call | 30-min founder/IR call | 5 days | 144 | 75% |
| 3. Full Data Room Access | NDA + financials | 7 days | 108 | 70% |
| 4. Management Meeting | Deep-dive with CEO/CTO/CFO | 10 days | 76 | 65% |
| 5. Technical Diligence | Product/architecture review | 12 days | 49 | 55% |
| 6. Term Sheet Issuance | Formal offer | 14 days | 27 | 80% |
| 7. Legal & Closing | SPA/SSA execution | 21 days | 22 | 100% (target) |

**Visual:** Stage-by-stage bar chart with aging buckets (e.g., 0–7 days, 8–14 days, >14 days) to flag bottlenecks.

---

#### Module 3: Term Sheet Negotiation Tracker
- **Active Term Sheets:** 3 (as of Week 4)
- **Key Negotiation Levers:**
  - Valuation & dilution (current ask: $120M pre; range $110M–$135M)
  - Liquidation preference (1x non-participating standard; 1.5x participating under discussion)
  - Board composition (2 founders, 1 independent, 1 investor)
  - Pro-rata rights & anti-dilution (weighted average vs. full ratchet)
  - Vesting schedules (4-year, 1-year cliff standard)
- **Term Sheet Status:** 1 in final signature; 2 in legal review
- **Negotiation Velocity:** Average 9 days from issuance to signed term sheet (target: ≤14 days)

**Visual:** Heatmap of negotiation terms vs. investor asks, with red/yellow/green status indicators.
