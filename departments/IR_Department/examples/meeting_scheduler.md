### I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Global AI Media Group operates at the intersection of frontier AI infrastructure and commercial media monetization. Our investor relations function must mirror the precision, scalability, and data-driven rigor of our core product. This protocol establishes a standardized, AI-orchestrated framework for managing the full investor lifecycle—from initial outreach to post-close relationship stewardship.

The system is designed to:
- Increase meeting-to-close conversion rates by **≥35%** within two quarters.
- Reduce administrative overhead by **60%** via automated briefing generation and follow-up sequencing.
- Provide a quantifiable relationship score for every investor counterparty, enabling dynamic resource allocation.

### II. PIPELINE STAGES & DEFINITIONS

We classify all investor interactions into five stages, each with distinct entry criteria and exit actions:

| Stage | Name | Entry Criteria | Exit Action |
|-------|------|----------------|-------------|
| 1 | **Prospecting** | Identified via market mapping, inbound inquiry, or AI-driven lead scoring (e.g., sector fit, AUM > $50M, prior AI infrastructure investments). | Schedule first call or send introductory deck. |
| 2 | **Qualification** | Completed initial 30-min call; confirmed mandate, check size range, and decision timeline. | Book deep-dive meeting; issue data room access. |
| 3 | **Deep-Dive** | Attended full management presentation (CEO/CFO/CTO); received live product demo of AI Workforce. | Send personalized follow-up packet within 24 hours. |
| 4 | **Negotiation** | Received term sheet interest or formal due diligence request. | Coordinate technical and financial DD sessions. |
| 5 | **Close & Stewardship** | Executed subscription agreement or equity investment. | Onboard to investor portal; schedule quarterly updates. |

### III. PRE-MEETING BRIEFING PACKETS (AI-GENERATED)

Every meeting—regardless of stage—must be preceded by a **Dynamic Briefing Packet (DBP)** generated by our internal AI system (IR-Bot 7, Department of Investor Relations). The DBP is auto-populated within **2 hours of meeting confirmation** and includes:

1. **Investor DNA Profile**  
   - Firm AUM, sector focus, geographic mandate, recent activity (last 5 deals), and stated thesis on AI infrastructure.  
   - Historical interaction log with Global AI Media Group (emails opened, content downloaded, prior meeting notes).

2. **Strategic Alignment Matrix**  
   - Mapping of investor’s portfolio themes to our 18 departments (e.g., if they back autonomous logistics, highlight our AI Supply Chain Unit).  
   - Three tailored talking points that bridge their thesis to our revenue trajectory.

3. **Financial Snapshot (Live)**  
   - Current ARR, Net Revenue Retention (NRR), Gross Margin, and CAC payback period—pulled from our internal ERP in real-time.  
   - Projected 18-month cash flow under base, bull, and bear scenarios.

4. **Competitive Positioning One-Pager**  
   - Comparison vs. legacy BPO providers and point-solution AI vendors (e.g., "We are not a chatbot; we are a full operating system with 224 employees.")  
   - Key differentiators: proprietary training data, department-level SLAs, and human-in-the-loop governance.

5. **Talking Points & Objection Handling**  
   - Pre-scripted responses to top 10 investor objections (e.g., "How is this different from outsourcing to India?" → "Our AI employees are 10x cheaper, 24/7 available, and never churn.")  
   - Each objection linked to a specific case study or metric.

### IV. POST-MEETING FOLLOW-UP TEMPLATES (AUTOMATED SEQUENCE)

Within **60 minutes** post-meeting, the IR-Bot triggers a personalized follow-up sequence based on meeting sentiment analysis (via voice/transcript NLP). Templates are modular and auto-filled:

**Template A — Qualified Interest (Score ≥ 70):**  
*Subject: "Next Steps: Deep-Dive Materials & Live Demo Access"*  
- Recap of three key discussion points (auto-summarized).  
- Link to personalized data room (NDA-gated).  
- Proposed calendar slots for technical deep-dive with our CTO.  
- Attach: 2-page executive summary, updated cap table
