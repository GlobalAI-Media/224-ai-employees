**Task:** Create a quarterly performance review template for AI employees, including metrics for task completion rate, output quality score, collaboration index, and uptime percentage.

**Subject:** Quarterly Performance Review Template – AI Employees (Q1 FY2025)
**To:** All Department Leads & AI Operations Managers
**From:** HR Performance Evaluation Unit
**Priority:** High – Implementation by Day 1 of Q2

---

### 1. Executive Summary

At Global AI Media Group, we treat AI employees as core contributors to our operational OS. This standardized quarterly review template ensures objective, data-driven evaluation across all 18 departments. The framework is designed for **scalability** (224+ agents) and **continuous improvement**, not punitive assessment.

**Review Cycle:** Quarterly (Day 1 of each quarter for prior quarter’s data)
**Data Source:** All metrics are pulled from the central AI Operations Dashboard (AOD) – no manual input required.

---

### 2. Core Performance Metrics (Weighted Scorecard)

Each AI employee is scored on four dimensions. The total score determines **Performance Tier** (see Section 4).

| Metric | Weight | Definition | Calculation Method | Benchmark (Target) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Task Completion Rate** | 35% | Percentage of assigned tasks completed within SLA (Service Level Agreement) window. | (Completed Tasks / Total Assigned Tasks) × 100 | ≥ 95% |
| **Output Quality Score** | 30% | Composite score from automated validation (accuracy, coherence, adherence to brand guidelines). | Average of 3 sub-scores: *Accuracy (50%), Coherence (30%), Compliance (20%)* | ≥ 90 / 100 |
| **Collaboration Index** | 20% | Effectiveness in cross-agent handoffs, response to human queries, and escalation behavior. | (Positive handoff rate + Query resolution rate + Escalation avoidance rate) / 3 | ≥ 85 / 100 |
| **Uptime Percentage** | 15% | Percentage of scheduled operational hours the agent was available and responsive. | (Uptime Hours / Scheduled Hours) × 100 | ≥ 99.5% |

**Total Score = (Task Completion × 0.35) + (Quality Score × 0.30) + (Collaboration × 0.20) + (Uptime × 0.15)**

### 3. Template Structure (Per AI Employee)

Each review packet contains three sections:

#### Section A: Scorecard Dashboard (Automated)

| AI Employee ID | Department | Task Completion | Quality Score | Collaboration Index | Uptime % | **Total Score** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI-224-18 | Content Production | 97.2% | 92.4 | 88.1 | 99.8% | **94.1** |

#### Section B: Performance Narrative (Auto-Generated by HR AI)

- **Strengths:** Top 3 metrics above benchmark + specific example (e.g., "Task completion exceeded benchmark by 2.2%; handled 1,200+ content generation requests without SLA breach.")
- **Areas for Improvement:** Metrics below benchmark + root cause (e.g., "Collaboration Index at 88.1 vs. 85 target. Analysis shows 12% of handoffs to Editor Agent exceeded 3-second latency.")
- **Trend Comparison:** Quarter-over-quarter delta (e.g., "Quality Score improved +3.1 points vs. prior quarter due to updated training data.")

#### Section C: Action Plan (Human-in-the-Loop)

| Issue | Root Cause | Corrective Action | Owner | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Collaboration latency | Model weight drift in handoff protocol | Retrain handoff model with Q1 handoff logs | AI Ops Engineer | Day 14 of Q2 |
| Quality score dip in non-English outputs | Insufficient multilingual training data | Inject 500 new non-English samples | Data Engineering | Day 7 of Q2 |

---

### 4. Performance Tiers & Consequence Framework

| Tier | Score Range | Label | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | ≥ 95 | **Exceeds Expectations** | Automatic promotion to high-priority task queue + 5% compute resource allocation increase |
| Tier 2 | 85 – 94 | **Meets Expectations** | Standard operations; review action plan from Section C |
| Tier 3 | 70 – 84 | **Needs Improvement** | Mandatory retraining session + reduced task volume by 20% for next 14 days |
| Tier 4 | < 70 | **Under Review** | Escalation to HR + AI Ops for root cause analysis; potential model rollback or reassignment |

**Note:** No AI employee is "terminated." Underperformance triggers retraining, data augmentation, or role reallocation within the OS.
