### Executive Summary
This report identifies three suitable UAE-based importers for a Brazilian sugar exporter, based on trade volume compatibility, logistical efficiency, and sector alignment. Brazil is the world’s largest sugar exporter, supplying over 20 million tonnes annually, while the UAE imports approximately 1.2 million tonnes of sugar per year (mostly raw and refined) for re-export, food processing, and local consumption. The UAE’s strategic location as a Gulf re-export hub and its zero-tariff sugar import policy make it an ideal market for Brazilian sugar. The recommended importers are selected for their high-volume procurement, established warehousing infrastructure in Jebel Ali Free Zone (JAFZA), and existing relationships with Brazilian suppliers.

### Key Matching Rationale

| Criteria | Brazilian Sugar Exporter Profile | UAE Importer Requirements |
|----------|----------------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Product Type** | Raw cane sugar (ICUMSA 600–800) and refined white sugar (ICUMSA 45) | Both raw for refining and refined for direct food/beverage use |
| **Volume Capacity** | 50,000–100,000 MT per shipment (FOB Santos) | Minimum 30,000 MT per contract, with ability to handle bulk cargo |
| **Logistics** | Santos port, 18–22 days transit to Jebel Ali | Deep-water port, cold storage, and silo capacity at JAFZA |
| **Payment Terms** | Letter of Credit (L/C) at sight, USD-denominated | Prefer L/C at sight or 30-day deferred payment |
| **Certifications** | ISO 22000, BRCGS, Fair Trade (optional) | Halal certification, UAE ESMA standards, re-export compliance |

- **Profile:** One of the largest sugar importers and refiners in the UAE, part of the Al Ghurair conglomerate. Operates a refinery in Dubai with capacity of over 500,000 MT per year.  
- **Matching Rationale:**  
  - High-volume buyer of Brazilian raw sugar (ICUMSA 600–800) for refining.  
  - Existing long-term contracts with Brazilian mills (e.g., Copersucar).  
  - Strong logistics network: silos at Jebel Ali, direct rail link to refinery.  
- **Cooperation Model:** **Long-term supply agreement (1–3 years)** with fixed quarterly volumes and price renegotiation based on ICE Sugar No. 11 futures. Recommend CIF Jebel Ali terms to align with their logistics.
