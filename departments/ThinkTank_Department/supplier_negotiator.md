**Trade Department Task: Supplier Negotiator – Negotiation Checklist for Vietnamese Coffee Supplier**

**Executive Summary**  
This checklist is designed to prepare your negotiation team for engaging with a Vietnamese coffee supplier. Vietnam is the world’s second-largest coffee exporter, primarily producing robusta beans (accounting for ~95% of output), with key growing regions in the Central Highlands (e.g., Dak Lak, Lam Dong). The checklist covers price terms, quality standards, and delivery timelines, with actionable items to secure a competitive, reliable supply agreement.

---

### 1. Pre-Negotiation Preparation

- **Market Intelligence**:  
  - Current robusta price benchmark: ICE Robusta futures (e.g., ~$2,800–$3,200/tonne as of Q3 2025); adjust for origin premiums.  
  - Vietnam’s export price range: typically $100–$300/tonne above futures for grade 1 robusta.  
  - Seasonality: Harvest runs October–January; prices dip post-harvest (Feb–May) and peak during dry season (June–Sept).  

- **Supplier Vetting**:  
  - Confirm supplier is a registered exporter (e.g., VICOFA member) with valid phytosanitary and fair-trade certifications.  
  - Request recent lab reports (SCAA or ISO 10470) for moisture content (max 12.5%), defect count, and screen size.  

- **Volume & Currency**:  
  - Define estimated annual volume (e.g., 20–50 tonnes) to negotiate tiered pricing.  
  - Preferred currency: USD (standard for Vietnamese coffee exports); lock exchange rate via forward contract if needed.

---

### 2. Price Terms Negotiation Checklist

| **Item** | **Action** | **Target Outcome** |
|----------|------------|-------------------|
| **Base Price** | Negotiate FOB (Ho Chi Minh City port) vs. CIF (your port). | FOB saves 3–5% on logistics; CIF shifts risk to supplier. |
| **Price Adjustment Clause** | Link to ICE Robusta futures (e.g., 90–95% of futures price + premium). | Cap premium at $150/tonne for grade 1. |
| **Volume Discount** | Propose 2% discount for orders >20 tonnes; 5% for >50 tonnes. | Secure at least 2% off base price. |
| **Payment Terms** | 30% deposit, 70% against shipping documents (L/C or T/T). | Avoid 100% upfront; target L/C at sight. |
| **Currency Fluctuation** | Include a clause for 2% buffer if USD/VND fluctuates >5% in 30 days. | Supplier absorbs first 2% change. |
| **Rebates & Penalties** | Late delivery penalty: 0.5% of invoice value per week (max 5%). | Enforce penalty; offer early payment rebate (1% if paid within 10 days). |

---

### 3. Quality Standards Negotiation Checklist

| **Parameter** | **Requirement** | **Negotiation Leverage** |
|---------------|-----------------|--------------------------|
| **Bean Grade** | Grade 1 robusta (screen 16+; defect count < 80 per 300g). | Reject Grade 2 (screen 13–15); demand lab certification. |
| **Moisture Content** | Max 12.5% (per Vietnam’s TCVN 4193 standard). | Penalty if >13%: price reduction of 1% per 0.5% excess. |
| **Defect Tolerance** | < 5% black beans; < 2% broken or insect-damaged. | Sample at origin (SGS or Bureau Veritas inspection). |
| **Roast Profile** | Specify if green (unroasted) or pre-roasted (e.g., medium roast). | Green beans allow you to control roasting; pre-roasted adds 10–15% premium. |
| **Certifications** | Require Rainforest Alliance, UTZ, or Fair Trade (if applicable). | Premium of 5–10% for certified beans; ensure traceability. |
