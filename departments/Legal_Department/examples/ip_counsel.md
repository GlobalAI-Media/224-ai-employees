### I. Executive Summary

The deployment of our AI Workforce (the "AI Employees") to generate trade documents, market analyses, and content raises critical questions regarding the ownership of the resulting output. Under current global legal frameworks, AI cannot be a legal "author" or "inventor." Consequently, ownership is determined by contractual allocation and the degree of human involvement in the creative or analytical process.

**Risk Rating: High (if unmanaged)** – Without explicit contractual terms, clients may claim full ownership, or third parties may challenge the validity of copyright/patent protections on AI-generated works, creating liability for our clients and reputational risk for our firm.

---

### II. Legal Analysis: Who Owns the Output?

**A. Copyright (Literary, Artistic, and Analytical Works)**

1.  **Traditional Doctrine (Berne Convention & National Laws):** Copyright vests in the "human author." Since AI is not a person, it cannot hold copyright.
2.  **Work-Made-For-Hire (US 17 U.S.C. § 101):** This doctrine applies to works prepared by an employee within the scope of employment. Our AI Employees are not legal employees. Therefore, this doctrine does not automatically apply.
3.  **UK & Commonwealth (CDPA 1988, s.9(3)):** In the UK, the author of a computer-generated work is "the person by whom the arrangements necessary for the creation of the work are undertaken." This is likely our company (the operator of the AI system), unless the contract states otherwise.
4.  **EU (CDSM Directive 2019/790):** The EU does not grant specific rights to AI-generated works; protection is limited to human intellectual creation. Therefore, without human "creative input" (e.g., substantial editing, selection, or arrangement), the work may fall into the public domain in the EU, exposing clients to free use by competitors.

**B. Trade Secrets & Confidential Information**

- **Market Analyses and Trade Documents** often contain proprietary client data and strategic insights. The *data* remains the client's property. However, the *compilation, analysis, and formatting* generated by the AI is a new work.
- **Risk:** If we do not assign rights, the client owns the raw data but may not have the exclusive right to the AI's specific analysis, potentially allowing us to use that analysis for other clients (a conflict of interest).

**C. Patents (Inventions)**

- **US (Alice/Mayo) & EPO (G 1/19):** AI cannot be named as an inventor. For a patent to be valid, a human must make a "significant contribution" to the conception of the invention. If the AI autonomously generates a novel process or product design, the patent may be invalid or unenforceable.

**D. Moral Rights**

- In jurisdictions recognizing moral rights (e.g., France, Germany), the "author" has rights of attribution and integrity. Since AI has no moral rights, these are extinguished. However, if a human claims authorship, they may retain these rights, potentially preventing clients from modifying the work.

### III. Recommended IP Clause for Client Contracts

The following clause is designed to allocate ownership clearly, protect our proprietary algorithms, and shield clients from third-party IP claims.

**Clause 8: Intellectual Property Rights in AI-Generated Output**

**8.1 Client Data.** All data, datasets, and confidential information provided by Client to Global AI Media Group ("Company") shall remain the sole property of Client. Company is granted a limited, non-exclusive, revocable license to use such data solely for the purpose of providing the Services.

**8.2 Output Ownership.**
(a) Subject to full payment of fees, Company hereby assigns to Client all rights, title, and interest in and to the **Deliverables** (defined as the final, human-reviewed trade documents, market analyses, and content specifically requested by Client and delivered in the final format).
(b) **Assignment of Rights:** To the extent permitted by law, Client shall be deemed the sole owner of the Deliverables. Company agrees to execute all documents reasonably requested to perfect this assignment.

**8.3 AI Tooling and Methodology.**
(a) **Reservation of Rights:** Company retains all right, title, and interest in and to the underlying AI models, algorithms, software, training data (excluding Client Data), and proprietary methodologies ("AI Tooling") used to generate the Deliverables.
