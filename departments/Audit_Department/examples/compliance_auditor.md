## compliance_auditor
**Task:** Design an internal audit framework for ensuring all 224 AI employees comply with operational policies, data handling procedures, and ethical guidelines. Include audit frequency, sampling methodology, and remediation workflow.

**Audit Memorandum – Internal Audit Framework: Compliance Audit of AI Employee Operations**

**To:** Audit Committee, Global AI Media Group  
**From:** Compliance Audit Division  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Internal Audit Framework for AI Employee Compliance – Design and Implementation Plan  

---

### 1. Audit Objectives

The primary objectives of this internal audit framework are to:

- Verify that all 224 AI employees adhere to documented operational policies, data handling procedures, and ethical guidelines.
- Assess the effectiveness of existing controls in preventing, detecting, and correcting non-compliance.
- Provide reasonable assurance to the Audit Committee and regulators regarding the integrity of AI-driven operations.
- Identify systemic risks and recommend control enhancements aligned with COSO Internal Control – Integrated Framework (2013) and IIA Standards (Attribute Standard 1000, Performance Standard 2100).

### 2. Scope and Coverage

- **Population:** All 224 AI employees across functional domains (content generation, data analytics, customer interaction, system administration).
- **In-Scope Policies:**  
  - Operational Policy Manual (OPM-2025)  
  - Data Handling Procedure (DHP-3.2)  
  - Ethical Guidelines for AI Agents (EGA-1.0)  
- **Exclusions:** Physical infrastructure and human employee compliance (covered by separate audits).

### 3. Audit Frequency

| Audit Type | Frequency | Rationale |
|------------|-----------|-----------|
| **Continuous Monitoring** | Real-time / Daily | Automated logging and anomaly detection for high-risk actions (e.g., data access, content output). |
| **Periodic Compliance Audit** | Quarterly | Sample-based review of policy adherence across all AI employees. |
| **Thematic Deep Dive** | Annually | Focused review of high-risk areas (e.g., ethical guideline breaches, data privacy incidents). |
| **Trigger-Based Audit** | Ad hoc | Initiated upon detection of material non-compliance, regulatory change, or whistleblower report. |

*Reference: IIA Performance Standard 2100 – Nature of Work; frequency aligns with risk assessment results.*

### 4. Sampling Methodology

**Approach:** Stratified Random Sampling with Proportional Allocation

- **Stratification Criteria:**  
  - Risk Tier (High, Medium, Low) based on historical compliance score and data sensitivity.  
  - Functional Domain (Content, Data, Customer, Admin).  
  - AI Employee ID (unique identifier).

- **Sample Size Calculation:**  
  - Confidence Level: 95%  
  - Margin of Error: ±5%  
  - Estimated Non-Compliance Rate: 5% (based on pilot data)  
  - **Minimum Sample per Quarter:** 60 AI employees (approximately 27% of population).  
  - **Stratified Allocation:**  
    - High Risk (n=20) – oversampled to ensure detection.  
    - Medium Risk (n=25) – proportional.  
    - Low Risk (n=15) – minimal but representative.

- **Selection Method:** Simple random sampling within each stratum using a cryptographically secure random number generator.

*Reference: IIA Practice Guide – Sampling Methodology; COSO Principle 10 – Selection and Development of Control Activities.*

### 5. Audit Procedures and Testing

**5.1 Operational Policy Compliance**
- **Test:** Review of AI employee action logs against policy checklists (e.g., approval workflows, escalation triggers).  
- **Evidence:** Automated log extraction, timestamp verification, policy rule matching.  
- **Sample:** 60 AI employees per quarter.

**5.2 Data Handling Procedures**
- **Test:** Verification of data access controls, encryption usage, and data retention/deletion schedules.  
- **Evidence:** Access control lists, encryption logs, data lifecycle reports.  
- **Sample:** 60 AI employees per quarter, with additional testing for those handling PII/PHI.

**5.3 Ethical Guidelines Adherence**
- **Test:** Review of content output for bias, misinformation, or prohibited topics; analysis of decision-making logs for ethical rule violations.  
- **Evidence:** Output samples, ethical rule violation flags, human review records.  
- **Sample:** 60 AI employees per quarter, supplemented by random output sampling (n=10 per AI employee).

### 6. Risk Ratings and Findings Classification

| Rating | Definition | Action Required |
|--------|------------|----------------|
| **High** | Material non-compliance with legal/regulatory requirements or core ethical guidelines. | Immediate remediation; escalate to Audit Committee within 24 hours. |
| **Medium** | Deviation from operational policy or data handling procedures without immediate legal risk. | Remediation within 30 days; report to management. |
| **Low** | Minor procedural errors or documentation gaps. | Remediation within 90 days; tracked in continuous improvement log. |
