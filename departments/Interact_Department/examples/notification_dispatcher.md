## 1. System Overview

The notification management system (NMS) is a high-throughput, multi-channel dispatcher designed to route operational, alerting, and user-facing notifications generated by 224 AI employees across the Global AI Media Group platform. The system must handle heterogeneous notification types, enforce delivery guarantees, and respect per-user and per-role preferences.

### 1.1 Core Requirements

- **Scale:** 224 AI employees, each generating up to 10 notifications/min in peak load → ~2,240 events/min (37.3 events/sec) baseline, with 10x burst factor → 373 events/sec sustained.
- **Latency:** P95 delivery decision < 50ms; channel handoff < 200ms.
- **Reliability:** At-least-once delivery with idempotent consumer semantics. No data loss on channel failure.
- **Observability:** Full traceability from generation to channel ack, with per-channel metrics and dead-letter queue (DLQ) inspection.

## 3. Notification Priority Levels

Five discrete priority levels, mapped to queue topics and delivery guarantees.

| Priority | Label    | Queue Topic | Max Latency Target | Retry Policy               | Channels Allowed          |
|----------|----------|-------------|--------------------|----------------------------|---------------------------|
| P0       | Critical | `notif.critical` | < 1s               | 5 retries, exponential backoff (1s→32s) | SMS, Push, In-App (always), Webhook |
| P1       | High     | `notif.high`     | < 5s               | 3 retries, exponential backoff (2s→8s)  | Push, In-App, Email (if pref) |
| P2       | Medium   | `notif.medium`   | < 30s              | 2 retries, fixed 10s interval           | In-App, Email, Webhook     |
| P3       | Low      | `notif.low`      | < 5min             | 1 retry, 60s delay                      | In-App only, Email digest  |
| P4       | Bulk     | `notif.bulk`     | < 1hr              | 0 retries (batch resend on failure)     | Email digest, In-App feed  |

**Rationale:** P0/P1 are reserved for system failures, security alerts, and user-facing account anomalies. P2–P4 are operational and informational.
